# The wake-up: night into dawn Chirho

Image-to-video between `town-night-chirho` (first frame) and `town-dawn-chirho` (last frame). The
site cannot play video, so frames from this clip become a sprite sheet that the hero scrubs as the
visitor scrolls: the first scroll on the page wakes the town up.

## Motion

Time-lapse of a tiny floating island town as night turns into dawn. The starry sky slowly
brightens, the stars and the Milky Way fade, and a soft rose and apricot glow rises along the
horizon behind the town. The shop windows light up one by one with warm golden light: first the
bakery in the centre, then the florist on the left, then the pottery studio on the right. The
lanterns on the steps glow and flicker gently, and the mist drifts slowly around the island. The
camera is completely still and locked off; the buildings, cobbles, steps and awnings never move or
change shape. Smooth, slow and magical, with no cuts.
