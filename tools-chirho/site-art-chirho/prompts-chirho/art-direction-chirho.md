# FIRST LIGHT: art direction for every generated image Chirho

One paragraph governs every image, so that they look like the work of one photographer. Each prompt
file in this folder starts with it, then adds its own scene.

## The paragraph (prepended to every prompt)

Cinematic, otherworldly and warm. Miniature tilt-shift photography of a tiny town that floats on a
soft island of cloud in a vast sky. It looks like a hand-built model photographed with a real lens:
shallow depth of field, a crisp centre, edges that melt softly; tactile real materials such as
painted timber, old brick, brass lamps, wavy hand-blown window glass and worn cobbles. Palette: deep
indigo night #0B1033 at the top of the sky, violet #4A2E6B, rose #C45E7C and apricot #F2A06B at the
horizon, warm window gold #F6C56B, and cool moonlight #8FB8FF on stone. The light is soft and
glowing, never harsh. No people's faces; any figures are tiny and seen from behind. No readable
text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere: shop signs are blank painted boards. No watermark, no
frame, no border.

## Why these choices

- **A floating town.** Every visitor knows a high street, and the floating island makes it
  otherworldly without making it cold. It is also the product's promise in one picture: a small
  business, lit up and findable.
- **Night into morning.** The page is one night becoming morning as you scroll: Secure in the
  night, Abundant at dawn, Easy in the morning. A shop owner's day starts in the dark.
- **Tilt-shift miniature.** It reads as crafted and precious, and its soft edges leave calm room
  for type.
- **No text in images.** Generated lettering is garbled, and any words belong to the page, where
  they can be read aloud, translated and corrected.
