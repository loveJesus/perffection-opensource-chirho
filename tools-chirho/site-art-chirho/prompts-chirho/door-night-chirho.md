# The door at night Chirho

The hero of the Trust page: the bakery's front door after closing, safe and watched over. A fresh
generation in the same palette as the night town.

## Scene (appended to the art-direction paragraph)

The bakery's front door at night in the floating town, close up at miniature scale. A painted timber
door, closed and secure, with a heavy brass lock and handle catching cool moonlight. A lit brass
lantern beside the door casts a warm pool of gold over the cobbled step, where a small cat sleeps
curled up. Through the window beside the door the shop is dark and tidy, with one tiny night light
glowing inside. Above, stars in a deep indigo sky and the soft edge of the cloud island. The feeling
is peace: everything locked, lit and watched over while the owner sleeps. The left third of the
frame is calm and dark, night sky and soft shadow, leaving room for words. No people. The sign board
is blank.
