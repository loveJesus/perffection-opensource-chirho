# The shop, inside, at first light Chirho

The hero of "What's in it": the same floating town's bakery, now seen from inside, as the day opens.
A fresh generation (not an edit), so the art-direction paragraph carries the palette and the lens.

## Scene (appended to the art-direction paragraph)

Inside the little bakery of the floating town, just after sunrise, photographed as a miniature at
eye level with the counter. A worn wooden counter with round loaves and pastries under glass domes,
a brass bell above the open door, shelves of jars and woven baskets, an apron on a hook, a few pots
of herbs on the window sill. Morning light streams in through wavy hand-blown window glass in soft
golden beams with drifting dust, and through the window the cloud island and a pale honey sky are
visible. Everything is ready and in its place, calm and welcoming before the first customer
arrives. The left third of the frame is calm and soft: a warm out-of-focus plaster wall in morning
light, leaving room for words. No people. Price tags, jars and the sign board are blank.
