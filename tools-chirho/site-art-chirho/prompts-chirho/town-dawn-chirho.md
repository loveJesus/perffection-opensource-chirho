# The hero: the town at the edge of dawn Chirho

Size 1536x1024, landscape. It becomes the hero still, and the MIDDLE of the wake-up sequence.

## Scene (appended to the art-direction paragraph)

A small cobbled high street of three independent shops on a gentle hill: a bakery in the centre
with a striped awning and loaves in its window, a florist on the left with buckets of flowers by the
door, and a pottery studio on the right with shelves of glazed bowls behind the glass. The street
sits on a floating island of soft cloud, with mist curling at its edges and a few lantern-lit steps
leading down into the cloud. It is the last moment of night: the top of the sky is deep indigo with
a handful of faint stars, melting through violet into a thin band of rose and apricot along the
horizon behind the town. Every shop window glows warm gold from within. The bakery is the brightest;
its door stands ajar and a pool of gold light spills across the cobbles. Composition: the town sits
in the lower right two-thirds of the frame, slightly off centre; the upper-left third is calm, open
sky with nothing in it, leaving room for a large headline.
